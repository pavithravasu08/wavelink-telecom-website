More about WaveLink broadband

What is WaveLink?

WaveLink is a fibre broadband service with unlimited data on every plan and speeds from 50 Mbps up to 1 Gbps. This is a student demo, so all plans and prices are fictional.

How does WaveLink fibre work?

A fibre cable runs all the way to your home and connects to a router. Fibre carries data as light, so speeds stay steady even when many devices are online.

Which speed do I need?

As a rough guide: 50 Mbps for 1–2 people, 100 Mbps for a small family, 300 Mbps for streaming, work calls and gaming together, and 500 Mbps or more for large households.

How long does installation take?

Installation is free. A technician visits within 24 hours and helps with router setup and Wi-Fi tuning.

How do I switch from another provider?

Switching takes about 3 days. We give up to ₹2,000 towards your old provider's exit fee as bill credit.
