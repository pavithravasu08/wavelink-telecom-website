Connect your device to the router, open any speed test website and run the test. For the best result, use a cable connection and close other apps while testing.

First restart your router (switch it off for 30 seconds, then on). Move closer to the router and disconnect devices you are not using. If it is still slow, contact customer service.

Open your router settings page in a browser, go to the Wi-Fi section, enter a new name or password and save. Your devices will need to reconnect with the new details.

Check that the set-top box is switched on and that the cable is fixed tightly at both ends. Make sure the TV input (HDMI/AV) matches the one your box is connected to.

Log in to your Wave Link account, open the TV section, choose the channel pack you want and confirm. New channels usually appear after the box refreshes.

Switch off the box, unplug it from the power socket, wait for one minute, then plug it in and switch it on again.

Log in to your Wave Link account, open the Mobile section, pick a plan and complete the payment. Your plan starts as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Insert the SIM, switch your phone on and follow the activation steps given with your SIM pack. Keep your ID details ready. If activation does not complete, contact customer service.

Turn on airplane mode for 10 seconds and turn it off again, then restart your phone. Also check that the SIM is placed properly in the tray.
