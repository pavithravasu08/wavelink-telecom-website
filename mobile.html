Mobile Plan FAQs

What is the difference between Prepaid and Postpaid?

Prepaid: you pay first and then use. Postpaid: you use first and pay a bill at the end of the month.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can switch to another plan when your current validity ends.

What happens when my daily data finishes?

Your internet speed goes down to a basic speed until the next day.
