Our Journey

Wave Link (WL) was created with a simple idea of bringing creativity, technology, and teamwork together to build meaningful digital experiences. Our journey began when six passionate Full Stack Developers came together with a common goal of learning, creating, and developing a complete web project. Each member contributed their own ideas, skills, and efforts throughout the development process. From planning the structure and designing the interface to developing, testing, and improving the website, our team worked together to turn our ideas into reality. The journey of Wave Link is not only about creating a website, but also about learning through collaboration, solving problems together, and continuously improving our technical skills. With teamwork, dedication, and a shared vision, WL continues to grow as a team of Full Stack Developers who believe that great ideas become better when they are built together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about WAVE LINK services.

Is broadband suitable for home use?

Yes. Broadband is suitable for browsing, online classes, video calls, streaming and connecting multiple devices.

Can I use broadband for online gaming?

Yes. A suitable broadband connection can support online gaming and other high-speed activities.

Can multiple devices connect to broadband?

Yes. Multiple devices such as phones, laptops, tablets and smart TVs can connect to a broadband network.

What should I do if my broadband is not working?

Check your router and connections first. If the issue continues, contact the support team for assistance.

Can I use TV and broadband together?

Yes. TV and broadband services can complement each other for a connected home entertainment experience.

Can I watch TV on different devices?

Depending on the available service and plan, TV content may be accessible on compatible devices.

How can I choose a mobile plan?

You can choose a plan based on your calling, messaging and mobile data requirements.

Can I use mobile data for internet access?

Yes. Mobile data allows you to access the internet on compatible smartphones and devices.

Can I use my mobile service while travelling?

Mobile service while travelling depends on network availability and the features included in your plan.

What is a network?

A network connects devices and allows them to communicate and share internet or other digital services.

Why is a reliable network important?

A reliable network helps provide smooth internet access, communication and better performance for connected devices.

What can affect network performance?

Network performance can be affected by connection quality, network traffic, device issues and coverage.

How can I improve my network connection?

Check your device and router connections, keep your equipment properly positioned and contact support if problems continue.

What should I do if my network is slow?

Check your connection and restart your network equipment. If the problem continues, contact the support team.
